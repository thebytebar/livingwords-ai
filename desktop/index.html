ABOUT LIVINGWORDS AI
Local desktop AI for serious work.

      
    

    

      
LivingWords AI is private desktop AI for serious users who need trusted workflows for writing, thinking, planning, and software tasks. Use it without an account, subscription, or per-prompt cloud fees.

      

        
Private by design

        
Responses are generated on this device and conversations are stored locally. Your prompts are not sent to a cloud AI service. You provide the hardware, storage, and power to run the model.

      

      

        
Powered by an open model

        
LivingWords AI includes Gemma 4 E2B Instruct, running locally. Model details and license information are available separately. Answers can be wrong and should be checked when accuracy matters.
